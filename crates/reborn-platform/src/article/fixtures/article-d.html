A school garden grows a record pumpkin

It weighed more than the head teacher.

A pumpkin grown by a primary school's gardening club has set a county record, weighing in at just over ninety kilograms.

The children watered it every morning before lessons and covered it with old blankets on the coldest nights of the year.
