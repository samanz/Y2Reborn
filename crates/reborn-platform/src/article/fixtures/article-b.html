Volunteers count the river's otters

By Jordan Sample

Tuesday, October 13, 2026 • 6:00 AM EDT

Dozens of volunteers spent the weekend walking the banks of the river at dawn, counting otters for the first survey of its kind in twenty years.

They found tracks and droppings along almost every stretch, and saw the animals themselves in eleven places.

"It felt like the river had been keeping a secret," said one of the walkers, who had lived beside it all her life without seeing one.

The results will go to the regional water board, which hopes to repeat the count every spring.
