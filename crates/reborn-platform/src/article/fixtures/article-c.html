How I built a kite from bin bags

October 13, 2026

Last spring my niece asked for a kite, and the only things in the shed were a roll of bin bags and some garden canes.

The bags turned out to be light and strong enough, which meant the first version flew on its very first afternoon.

Materials

  • Three heavy bin bags, opened flat
  • Two bamboo garden canes
  • A reel of strong kite line
ratio: tail three times the height of the kite

Taping the edges took the longest, mostly because the wind kept lifting the plastic off the table.

Wind always finds the weak seam.

If you try this, fly it on a beach. Trees win every time.
