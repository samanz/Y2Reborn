Town library reopens after a long repair

Riley WriterArts reporter

The town library will reopen on Monday after eighteen months of repairs to its roof and windows, the council has said.

Builders found the old timber frame in better condition than expected, which kept the cost below the first estimate and let the work end early.

"People have asked about it every single week," said the head librarian, who moved the books to a church hall while the work went on.

Opening hours

The library will open from nine until six on weekdays, with a late evening on Thursdays for the reading group and the homework club.
